## The spine

Everything in this corpus descends from two sentences colliding:

> **Capability grows fastest where verification is cheap.**
> **Value accrues to whatever intelligence cannot manufacture.**

The first is a claim about the technology, forced by [data exhaustion](../01-substrate/data.md) pushing the frontier onto verifiable-reward training. The second is a claim about economics, forced by [inference cost collapse](../01-substrate/inference-economics.md) making cognition itself abundant.

The first explains the *uneven* progress across [every domain](../03-domains/) — why math and code raced ahead of taste and judgment. The second explains *where the money goes* — to energy, land, licenses, distribution, trust, and physical presence rather than to the technology or to most of the firms adopting it.

Together they produce the document's least intuitive conclusion: **the transition is real, large, and mostly invisible in the aggregate statistics**, because competitive markets pass the gains to consumers and the concentrated harms land on cohorts and regions rather than on national averages.
